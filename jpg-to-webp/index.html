Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about converting JPG to WebP.

Is the JPG to WebP converter free?

Yes. You can convert JPG and JPEG images to WebP completely free with no registration, no subscription fees, and no watermark added.

Are my JPG images uploaded to any cloud server?

No. Supported images are re-encoded locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas APIs. Zero image files are ever uploaded across the network.

Why should I convert JPG to WebP for my website?

WebP produces photographic files that are 25% to 35% smaller than equivalent JPGs at identical visual quality, drastically speeding up page load times and passing Google Core Web Vitals.

Does converting JPG to WebP cause noticeable quality loss?

No. WebP uses advanced predictive block intra-frame coding that prevents the blocky pixel artifacts commonly seen in aggressive JPG compression, preserving smooth gradients and textures.

Can I convert multiple JPG photos at the same time?

Yes. You can batch convert up to 20 images at once (up to 50 MB each) directly in your browser with zero server queue wait times.
