The Music Subscription Trap: Why Creators Look for Alternatives

  • Music Only: Epidemic Sound does not provide B-roll stock footage, forcing creators to purchase a second subscription for stock video (e.g., Storyblocks or Envato Elements).

The Legal Superpower of Creative Commons & Public Domain

Many creators don't realize that Creative Commons licenses offer a significant legal advantage over private subscription contracts:

Creative Commons licenses are legally irrevocable. Under Section 2(a)(1) of the official Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0 legal code, once a copyright owner releases audio under a Creative Commons license, they cannot retroactively revoke that license or claim copyright infringement against someone who followed the terms.

In contrast, SaaS platforms like Epidemic Sound grant a conditional, temporary license tethered to your recurring monthly payments. If your credit card expires or you pause your membership, your right to use that audio in future projects vanishes immediately.

The Safe YouTube Attribution Blueprint

To ensure 100% monetization safety and prevent erroneous copyright claims on YouTube when using Creative Commons audio, follow this simple standard:

  1. For CC0 / Public Domain Tracks: No attribution is legally required. You can monetize immediately with zero description credits.
  2. For CC-BY (Attribution) Tracks: Paste a clean 1-line credit into your YouTube video description. For example:
    Sound: "[Sound Name]" by [Creator] licensed under CC BY 4.0 ([Direct URL])
  3. One-Click Attribution Generator: When you browse sounds on CreatorNew Free Sounds, click the "Copy Attribution" button to grab the exact formatted text ready for your video description.
