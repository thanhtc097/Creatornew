How to Prevent ChatGPT From Sounding Like an AI Bot

Every experienced reader can spot unedited ChatGPT text instantly: excessive use of the word "delve," rhetorical questions like "Have you ever wondered?", and trite concluding paragraphs beginning with "In conclusion."

To purge this from your outputs, include this Style Cleansing Directive in your custom instructions or prompt:

Style Rules:
1. Ban these words: delve, testament, crucial, realm, leverage, landscape, paramount, beacon.
2. Write in active voice only. Eliminate passive constructions.
3. Vary sentence lengths drastically: combine 4-word punchy statements with longer rhythmic explanations.
4. Never summarize the prompt back to me. Dive straight into the response.

The 10x Repurposing Prompt: One Idea, Five Assets

I am providing a core idea or transcript below. Your task is to transform it into 4 distinct distribution assets:
1. A viral LinkedIn post formatted with high white-space readability
2. A 5-tweet thread with a compelling curiosity-gap hook
3. A 60-second vertical video script with visual b-roll cues in brackets
4. A 2-sentence newsletter teaser leading to a call-to-action link

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best custom instruction setting for ChatGPT?

Set your profile instructions to specify your brand tone, target audience, reading level (8th grade is ideal for web), and a list of banned buzzwords.

Can ChatGPT write full long-form articles in one shot?

It is far better to generate content iteratively: first create an outline, review and edit the sections, and then prompt the model to draft section by section.

Does Google penalize content written with ChatGPT?

Google’s official stance states that content is judged on helpfulness, accuracy, and user satisfaction (E-E-A-T), not on whether AI was used in its creation.
