Instagram Dimensions (Feed, Stories, Reels)

On Instagram, vertical screen estate equals engagement. Avoid 1:1 square posts when possible in favor of 4:5 vertical portrait posts:

  • Portrait Post (Recommended): 1080 × 1350 px (4:5). Gives your post 25% more vertical viewport space on mobile feeds, slowing users' thumbs.
  • Square Post: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1). Safe standard, but captures less screen space.
  • Stories & Reels: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16). Leave top 220px and bottom 280px clear of critical text to prevent obstruction by account handles and UI buttons.
  • Profile Picture: 320 × 320 px. Remember Instagram displays avatars in a circular mask.

YouTube Dimensions: Thumbnails & Banner Safe Zones

YouTube thumbnails directly determine your Click-Through Rate (CTR):

  • Video Thumbnail: 1280 × 720 px (16:9 minimum). Must remain under the strict 2MB file limit. Use our Image Compressor if your high-detail thumbnail exceeds 2MB. Keep critical text away from the bottom-right corner where video timestamps overlay.
  • Channel Banner: 2560 × 1440 px. YouTube banners render across mobile, tablets, desktop, and 4K smart TVs. Place your logo, tagline, and schedule inside the central 1232 × 338 px "Safe Area" to avoid mobile edge cropping.

TikTok & Facebook Sizing Rules

  • TikTok Fullscreen Video / Photo Mode: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16). TikTok Photo Mode carousels have exploded in popularity; ensure all slides adhere strictly to 9:16.
  • Facebook Feed Post & Link Share: 1200 × 630 px (1.91:1). This is also the universal OpenGraph (og:image) standard used by social crawlers when sharing web links.
  • Facebook Page Cover: 820 × 312 px on desktop, displayed as 640 × 360 px on mobile smartphones.

X (Twitter) & LinkedIn Professional Sizing

  • X (Twitter) Feed Post: 1200 × 675 px (16:9). Images outside standard aspect ratios may be center-cropped on mobile feeds.
  • X Header Banner: 1500 × 500 px (3:1). Remember that your profile avatar will overlap the bottom-left corner.
  • LinkedIn In-Feed Post: 1200 × 627 px. For infographics or PDF carousels, 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) is strongly favored.
  • LinkedIn Personal Banner: 1584 × 396 px (4:1).

The Real Reason Your Social Uploads Look Blurry

Creators often assume: "If I upload my raw 48-megapixel camera shot (8000x6000 px), the platform will display the sharpest possible version."

In reality, the opposite happens:

  1. Aggressive Server Resampling: When you upload a massive 15MB file, platform CDNs apply harsh automated bicubic downsampling, degrading subtle contrast and softening sharp edges.
  2. Color Profile Shift: Social networks expect the sRGB color profile. If you export in Adobe RGB or ProPhoto RGB, colors will appear washed out and muddy.
  3. File Size Thresholds: Files exceeding thresholds are severely recompressed into low-bitrate JPGs.

The Solution: Pre-scale your image to the exact target pixels before uploading. If Instagram wants 1080x1350, give it exactly 1080x1350. The CDN will pass your image through with minimal re-encoding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best resolution for Instagram posts in 2026?

The best resolution is 1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5 aspect ratio). This vertical format occupies the maximum available screen height on mobile feeds without being cropped by Instagram's UI.

How do I prevent my YouTube thumbnail from exceeding 2MB?

Export your thumbnail at exactly 1280x720 pixels, then run it through our free client-side Image Compressor at 82%–85% quality. This will drop typical file weights from 4MB down to 350KB with zero noticeable quality difference.

Can I use WebP images on social media?

While web browsers universally support WebP, some social upload forms (like LinkedIn or older Facebook dashboards) still enforce JPG or PNG extensions. If a platform rejects WebP, use our free WebP to JPG converter before uploading.
