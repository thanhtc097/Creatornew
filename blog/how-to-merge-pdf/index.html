1. Common Scenarios Requiring PDF Merging

Combining separate documents into a single consolidated PDF is standard across many professional workflows:

  • Job & Visa Applications: Merging resumes, cover letters, references, and portfolios into a single cohesive document.
  • Financial & Legal Records: Bundling monthly statements, contract appendices, and signed receipts.
  • Academic Submissions: Stitching title pages, essays, charts, and bibliographies into a unified assignment file.

2. How Client-Side Merging Preserves Document Quality

Traditional PDF merging tools often re-render pages as raster images before stitching, resulting in fuzzy text and blurry line art. In contrast, CreatorNew combines the internal PDF page dictionary objects and font tables directly. Vector paths remain mathematical formulas, fonts stay embedded, and text remains sharp at any zoom level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does merging PDFs degrade the quality of embedded text or images?

No. Client-side merging combines the underlying PDF page streams and font dictionaries directly without recompressing or rasterizing vector text or images.

Is there a file size limit for merging PDFs on CreatorNew?

Because processing happens locally on your computer using WebAssembly, there are no artificial server upload limits. Processing capability depends on your local device RAM.

Are my merged documents uploaded to any remote server?

No. All PDF operations run locally in browser memory. Documents are never transmitted to CreatorNew servers or third parties.
