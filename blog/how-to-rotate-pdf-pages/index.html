Why "Rotate View" in Your PDF Reader Does Not Save the File

Many users open a sideways PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader or Apple Preview, click the "Rotate" view icon, and assume the file is fixed. However, closing the document reverts it back to its original sideways state!

This happens because "Rotate View" only rotates your local display screen without updating the internal /Rotate attribute in the PDF’s page dictionary. To fix the orientation permanently, you must modify the underlying metadata with a tool like CreatorNew.
