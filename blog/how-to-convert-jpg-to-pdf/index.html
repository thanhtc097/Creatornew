Why PDFs Are Better Than Loose JPGs for Document Sharing

Sharing multiple loose JPG images creates friction:

  • Email attachments get scattered or rejected due to file count limits.
  • Recipients have to open each file separately, often displaying out of order.
  • Printers require manual page setup for each individual image.

Converting your images into a single structured PDF guarantees that your pages appear in the exact order you intended, prints predictably on any standard printer, and looks thoroughly professional.
