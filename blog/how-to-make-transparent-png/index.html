Understanding the Checkerboard Pattern: What Is an Alpha Channel?

In graphic design software and photo editors, a grey-and-white checkerboard grid does not mean the image has checks—it is the universal digital visual representation of empty space (transparency).

An alpha channel stores transparency information alongside Red, Green, and Blue color data. While a 1-bit format (like GIF) only knows whether a pixel is 100% solid or 100% invisible (causing harsh jagged edges), an 8-bit PNG alpha channel provides 256 gradations of translucency, allowing soft drop shadows and feathered hair to blend naturally onto any backdrop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my transparent PNG turn black when I saved it or sent it via email?

Some older image viewers, email clients, or messaging apps do not support alpha channel previews and render empty pixels as solid black. The transparency remains fully intact when opened in design tools.

Can I convert a transparent PNG to JPG while keeping the transparency?

No. The JPEG standard cannot support transparency. Converting a transparent PNG to JPG will automatically fill the background with solid white or black.
