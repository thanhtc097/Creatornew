Why Split PDFs Locally Instead of Cloud Uploading?

Large PDF documents frequently contain sensitive personal data—such as employee payroll files, scanned client tax records, or medical charts. When you use conventional online splitters, you risk leaking confidential pages to remote servers.

By using CreatorNew’s client-side Split PDF Tool, the splitting and page extraction operations happen strictly in local RAM via WebAssembly. Your documents are never exposed to the web.

Step-by-Step: Extracting Pages from Any PDF

  1. Navigate to the Split PDF Tool.
  2. Upload your PDF file. Interactive visual page thumbnails will load instantly.
  3. Choose your extraction method: enter page numbers (e.g. 1-3, 7, 9-12) or click directly on the thumbnails you wish to keep.
  4. Click "Extract Pages" to generate your new, streamlined PDF document immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will splitting a PDF degrade its visual sharpness?

No. The splitting process losslessly copies the exact PDF vector streams, font definitions, and embedded image assets into a new container without modification.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages (e.g., page 2, 5, and 11)?

Yes! Simply specify your desired page list separated by commas, and our tool will bundle only those exact pages into your new file.

Can I split password-protected PDFs?

If a PDF has an open-password, you must enter the password to decrypt it before pages can be extracted.
