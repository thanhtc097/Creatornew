How Cutout Quality Compares on Tricky Edges

Background segmentation algorithms are tested by three notoriously difficult edge types:

  • Fine Strands of Hair & Fur: Cheap algorithms create a sharp plastic 'helmet' effect. CreatorNew's deep neural network calculates soft alpha masks that preserve individual hair strands and animal fur.
  • Transparent & Semi-Reflective Glass: Capturing clear glass bottles or spectacles requires multi-level alpha blending rather than a binary cut.
  • Complex Shadows: Separating a subject's natural ambient shadow from a solid backdrop determines whether a cutout looks professional or artificial.
