The Hidden Security Risks of Cloud PDF Mergers

Most people do not realize that standard online PDF tools require you to upload your files to remote web servers. If you are merging tax returns, bank statements, legal contracts, or medical records, uploading them to unknown third-party servers presents severe security and compliance liabilities (including GDPR and HIPAA concerns).

CreatorNew operates entirely on client-side WebAssembly technology. When you drag PDFs into our tool, your browser processes the PDF structure locally in your computer’s memory. Your files are never uploaded across the internet, ensuring 100% confidentiality.
