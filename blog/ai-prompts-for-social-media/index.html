Platform-Specific Prompt Blueprints

1. The Viral LinkedIn Discussion Starter

Act as a B2B marketing thought leader. Write a LinkedIn post based on this core insight: [INSERT TOPIC].
Requirements:
- First line must be a contrarian observation under 10 words that challenges common industry wisdom.
- Follow with a 3-sentence personal anecdote illustrating why the common wisdom fails.
- Provide a 3-step modern alternative.
- Conclude with an open question encouraging comments.
- Do not use hashtags or emojis.

2. The High-Bookmark X Thread Generator

Write a 6-tweet thread breaking down [COMPLEX SUBJECT] for beginners.
- Tweet 1: A compelling hook stating why mastering this skill saves 10 hours a week.
- Tweets 2-5: Each tweet covers one actionable tactic with a real-world example.
- Tweet 6: A summary tweet with a call-to-action to repost and bookmark the thread.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I use AI to generate social media content?

AI is most effective as an ideation partner and first-draft generator. Always infuse personal anecdotes, real experiences, and proprietary screenshots to make content uniquely yours.

Which prompt structure gets the most comments on LinkedIn?

Contrarian viewpoints that politely challenge popular industry myths consistently generate the highest comment volumes.

Can AI generate hashtags for my posts?

Yes, but in 2026, social algorithms rely primarily on semantic keyword search in post text rather than hashtags. Focus on keyword-rich copy instead of hashtag stuffing.
