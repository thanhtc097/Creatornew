The 5-Part Formula for Photorealistic AI Images

To create realistic, cinematic photography rather than obvious digital illustrations, structure your prompts using this 5-stage formula:

  1. Subject Description: Specific details about age, ethnicity, expression, attire, and posture (e.g., "A weathered 50-year-old carpenter inspecting a wooden joint with intense focus").
  2. Environment & Background: Realistic setting with textural depth (e.g., "In a sunlit rustic woodworking workshop with sawdust particles floating in the air").
  3. Lighting & Mood: Natural lighting source (e.g., "Dramatic directional morning sunlight streaming through dusty industrial windows").
  4. Camera & Technical Specs: Camera body, lens length, and aperture (e.g., "35mm film photography, Kodak Portra 400, natural color grading, soft vignette").
  5. Parameters: Aspect ratio and stylize flags (e.g., --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do AI models struggle with rendering realistic hands and fingers?

Hands are articulated in hundreds of complex 3D poses that are frequently occluded in 2D training data. Modern engines like Midjourney and Flux have dramatically improved anatomical accuracy.

Can I generate an image with transparent background in Midjourney?

No. Midjourney renders a solid raster pixel canvas. Generate your subject on a simple plain backdrop and use CreatorNew Background Remover to create a true transparent PNG.

What is the best aspect ratio for YouTube thumbnails in Midjourney?

Always append --ar 16:9 to your prompt in Midjourney to generate native 16:9 images suitable for 1280x720 thumbnail sizing.
