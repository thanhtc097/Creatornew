Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to PNG free?

Yes. CreatorNew PDF to PNG is 100% free with no registration, subscriptions, or watermarks.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. The entire rendering and conversion process runs client-side in your web browser. Your confidential documents never touch any external server.

Why choose PNG over JPG for PDF conversion?

PNG is a lossless format. It preserves exact text sharpness, line art, diagrams, and transparent graphical elements without lossy compression artifacts.

Can I convert multiple pages and download as a ZIP?

Yes. If multiple pages are converted, all PNG files are bundled automatically into a single ZIP file for easy one-click download.
