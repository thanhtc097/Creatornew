Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CreatorNew's private artificial intelligence utilities.

How does CreatorNew run AI models without server uploads?

CreatorNew uses ONNX Runtime Web and WebAssembly to execute lightweight computer vision models directly on your computer's GPU or CPU. Once the model weights are loaded into your browser cache, processing happens 100% locally with zero data sent to external servers.

Are there credit limits or paywalls on CreatorNew AI tools?

No. Unlike commercial SaaS tools (e.g. remove.bg or Canva) that charge per image or cap free exports at low resolution, CreatorNew is completely free with unlimited high-resolution exports.

Which AI models does the Prompt Builder support?

The AI Prompt Builder supports leading language and vision models, including OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude (with XML formatting), Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Midjourney, and Flux.

Are my prompts or generated texts stored or shared?

No. All prompt drafting, variable assembly, and copying take place solely inside your web browser. Nothing is logged or shared.

Do I need a high-end graphics card to run these AI tools?

No. Our on-device neural networks are quantized for fast execution on standard laptops, desktop processors, and modern mobile devices without requiring a dedicated graphics card.
