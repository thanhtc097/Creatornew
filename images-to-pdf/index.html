Frequently asked questions

Is the Images to PDF converter free?

Yes. It is completely free and requires no registration, subscription, or watermark.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server?

No. Your images are converted to PDF pages locally in your browser's private memory sandbox with zero network uploads.

Which image formats can I convert to PDF?

You can add and combine JPG, PNG, and modern WebP images into a single PDF document.

Can I choose between A4 and US Letter page sizes?

Yes. You can select standard international A4 or US Letter page dimensions, customize orientation (Portrait, Landscape, or Auto), and adjust margin sizes.

How many images can I convert into one PDF?

You can combine up to 30 images in a single session directly in your web browser.
