Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our free, on-device AI background and object removal tool.

Is CreatorNew AI Background Remover free?

Yes. You can remove backgrounds and erase unwanted objects completely free without subscriptions, account registration, or watermarks. There are no hidden fees or paywalls.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server?

No. The AI model executes entirely inside your browser via WebAssembly (WASM). Your photos are processed exclusively in volatile device RAM and never leave your computer or phone.

Why does the first use take a few seconds to load?

Your browser downloads the lightweight neural network model weights during your first visit. Once downloaded, your browser caches the model locally, allowing subsequent edits to execute almost instantaneously—even when disconnected from the internet.

What image formats are supported?

CreatorNew supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP input images up to 25 MB. Cutouts are automatically exported as 32-bit transparent PNG files preserving full color accuracy and alpha transparency.

How does CreatorNew compare to Remove.bg?

While Remove.bg is a cloud-based service that restricts free users to tiny 0.25 MP preview images and charges recurring subscription fees for HD downloads, CreatorNew is 100% free, exports at original full resolution, and guarantees absolute privacy by processing client-side.

Can I remove unwanted objects or people from images?

Yes. Switch to the 'Erase owned logo/object' tab at the top of the workspace. Paint a mask over any unwanted tourist, powerline, or object, and our on-device deep learning inpainting AI will seamlessly reconstruct the background.
