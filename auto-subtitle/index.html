Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI auto subtitles

Is this AI subtitle generator completely free?

Yes! CreatorNew Auto Subtitle Generator is 100% free with no watermark, no signup, and no subscription paywalls. You can export as many videos and subtitle files as you need.

Why is word-level karaoke highlighting important for short videos?

Studies show that over 80% of users watch TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts on mute or in busy environments. Animated word-by-word bouncing captions increase retention and average watch time dramatically.

Are my private videos uploaded to external servers?

No. Video playback, canvas overlays, and hardcoded video recording happen entirely on your device via HTML5 Web APIs. When using Gemini AI mode, only downsampled audio is sent to the AI API for speech transcription.

What is the TikTok / Shorts Safe Zone?

Social media apps overlay action buttons (Like, Comment, Share, Sound disk) on the right side and bottom 20% of vertical videos. Our Safe Zone guide ensures your subtitles stay centered and readable without being obscured.
