Frequently asked questions

Is the AI Prompt Builder free?

Yes. You can generate, edit, copy and download prompts without registration.

Does CreatorNew send my prompt to an AI service?

No. This tool structures your prompt locally in the browser. You choose where to use it.

Does the tool generate AI answers?

No. It creates a detailed prompt that you can paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another AI tool.

Can I edit the generated prompt?

Yes. The generated prompt remains fully editable before you copy or download it.
