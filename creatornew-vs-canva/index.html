The Canva Dilemma: Essential Features Locked Behind a Paywall

Canva is an extraordinary template and design tool used by millions of small business owners, social media managers, and creators worldwide. However, users on Canva's free plan quickly hit painful roadblocks when working with images:

  • Background Remover Is Pro-Only: Canva's one-click "Background Remover" and "Magic Eraser" are strictly reserved for Canva Pro subscribers, costing $14.99/month or $119.99/year. Clicking the button on a free account triggers a modal requiring your credit card.
