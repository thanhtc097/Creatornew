Trust & Data Protection

CreatorNew Privacy Policy

Your files belong to you. We built our entire platform around on-device processing so that your sensitive images, legal documents, and personal files never leave your computer.

Effective Date: September 2026 · Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. The Foundation: Zero Server Upload Guarantee

The most important principle of CreatorNew is simple: We do not collect, view, store, transmit, or process your files on our servers.

When you use any of our image or document utilities:

  • Image Tools (Image Converter, Image Compressor, Image Resizer, PNG to WebP, JPG to WebP, WebP to JPG): All decompression, pixel transformations, and re-encoding occur entirely within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and native browser APIs.
  • PDF Tools (Merge PDF, Split PDF, Delete PDF Pages, Images to PDF, PDF to JPG, PDF to PNG, Rotate PDF): Document parsing, page manipulation, and PDF generation are handled client-side using JavaScript in volatile browser memory.
  • AI Background & Object Remover: AI inference runs locally on your machine via WebAssembly (WASM) and ONNX Runtime Web. The model runs on your CPU inside a Web Worker. No pixels are ever uploaded to a remote AI cloud API.

Because your files are processed in your browser's local RAM, you can even disconnect your internet connection once the page is loaded, and the conversion or editing will complete without error.

2. What Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or store your original images, photos, or graphics.
  • We do not collect or store your PDFs or confidential document contents.
  • We do not require user registration, passwords, email addresses, or payment card details.
  • We do not use facial recognition, biometric analysis, or image indexing on your files.
  • We do not retain or sell any user data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

3. Local Browser Storage & Caching

CreatorNew uses standard browser storage mechanisms solely to improve your user experience and performance:

  • Language Preference: We store a small preference key (creatornew-language) in your browser's localStorage so that the site remembers your chosen language (English or Vietnamese) across visits.
  • AI Model Caching: When you use the AI Background Remover for the first time, your browser downloads the ONNX neural network model weights and stores them in your browser's Cache API or IndexedDB. This ensures that on future visits, the model loads instantly without consuming your network bandwidth.

All data stored in your browser remains on your physical device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Media APIs

CreatorNew provides search discovery tools for openly licensed media:

  • Free Sounds: Queries the public Openverse API to discover Creative Commons audio. When you perform a search, your search query text (for example, "acoustic guitar") is sent directly from your browser to the Openverse API to fetch search results.
  • Free Videos: Queries the public Wikimedia Commons API using your search keyword.

Neither of these tools uploads personal files or tracks your identity. They only dispatch your text search term to fetch public media catalogs.

5. Web Hosting & Technical Logs

Like virtually all websites, when your web browser requests a page or asset (such as an HTML document, stylesheet, or icon) from our web hosting infrastructure, the web server automatically logs standard technical metadata:

  • Your IP address and general geographic region.
  • Your browser type and operating system (User-Agent string).
  • The referring URL and timestamp of the request.

These server logs are used strictly for network security, DDoS prevention, troubleshooting server errors, and monitoring aggregate site traffic. Server logs do not contain and cannot capture the files you manipulate inside our tools.

6. Analytics & Cookies

CreatorNew strives to maintain a minimal, privacy-respecting footprint. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies. Any analytics deployed are strictly aggregated and anonymized to understand general visitor counts, popular pages, and performance metrics, without profiling individual users.

7. GDPR and CCPA / CPRA Compliance

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right of Access & Portability: Because we do not store your personal files or link activity to personal identities, we have no personal data profiles to export.
  • Right of Erasure: Your files never reside on our servers, so there is no remote data to delete. Simply closing your browser tab permanently purges the working files from your device RAM.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or file content to any third parties under any circumstances.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection

CreatorNew is designed as a safe, neutral utility for students, creators, and professionals of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since our tools require no account registration, children can use our image and document utilities without sharing personal details.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new tools, enhanced browser capabilities, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
