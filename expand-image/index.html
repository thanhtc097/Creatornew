Practical Tips

Tips for Better Outpainting Results & Troubleshooting

Follow these practical guidelines for clean background extensions.

💡 BEST PRACTICES
  • Select Natural Textures: Skies, foliage, studio backdrops, oceans, textures, and architecture outpaint with high visual continuity.
  • Multi-Pass Expansion: For extreme canvas expansions (e.g. 1:1 to 21:9), expand first to 16:9, click Continue Editing, and expand once more for cleaner density.
  • Retouch with Inpaint Brush: Paint over unwanted background objects or edge artifacts before expanding so the AI cleans and uncrops in one step.
  • Inspect with Zoom 100%: Use the split-slider and 100% zoom button to inspect transition seams before exporting.
🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING
  • Visible Transition Line: Painting a thin brush stroke along the boundary line triggers the neural model to re-blend the transition softly.
  • Repetitive Patterns: If a texture repeats in empty background space, paint a light brush stroke across the repeated section and click Re-Expand.
  • Cut-Off Hands or Faces: Outpainting is designed for background extrapolation. Keep human subjects slightly inside the canvas edges rather than cut off at the border.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About AI Image Expander

How do I change a vertical photo to horizontal without cropping?

Upload your vertical photo, select the 16:9 widescreen preset, and click Expand. The on-device neural network generates natural scenery on the left and right margins to fit horizontal displays without cropping or stretching your original subject.

Can I extend an image background for free without signing up?

Yes. CreatorNew is completely free to use directly in your browser. There is no account registration, no credit deduction, and no watermark added to your exported images.

Does this AI expander work offline?

Yes. Once the lightweight model (92.6 MB) is stored in your browser's cache after first use, you can disconnect from the internet and continue uncropping photos completely offline.

What is the difference between outpainting, uncropping and inpainting?

Outpainting and uncropping both refer to generating new background content beyond the original outer borders of a photo. Inpainting refers to modifying or removing objects inside the original canvas using a brush. CreatorNew combines both capabilities in a single pass.

Why does my expanded image show repeating patterns or seams?

Repeating textures or visible seams can occur when expanding complex or cut-off objects. To fix this, enable the Inpaint Brush, paint lightly over the seam or repetitive area, and click Re-Expand to blend the transition naturally.

Does it work on phone and which browsers support WebGPU?

Yes, it works on both desktop and mobile. Modern browsers including Chrome, Edge, Safari 18+, and Firefox support on-device execution. WebGPU accelerates rendering on compatible GPUs, while WebAssembly (WASM SIMD) provides a reliable fallback for older devices.
