Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI Image Expander

What is AI Uncropping or Outpainting?

Outpainting extends an image beyond its original canvas boundaries. Rather than cropping or stretching your photo, the AI model generates realistic extensions of the background, sky, room, or scenery to fill the new space.

How does Generative Fill work alongside expansion?

If you want to remove an unwanted tourist, watermark, or object while expanding the canvas, activate the Inpaint Brush and paint over that area. The neural network will inpaint the brushed area and synthesize the outer borders in a single unified pass.

Why does the first use take a few moments to load?

On your first visit, your browser caches the lightweight neural model weights (92.6 MB). After the first run, the model remains cached in your browser and executes instantly, even offline.
