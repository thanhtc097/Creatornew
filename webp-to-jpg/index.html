Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about converting WebP to JPG.

Is the WebP to JPG converter free?

Yes. You can convert WebP images to JPG completely free with no registration, no subscription fees, and no watermark added.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server?

No. All image decoding and re-encoding executes locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Zero data is uploaded to CreatorNew or third-party servers.

Why should I convert WebP to JPG?

JPG is universally supported across older image editing software, office applications, operating systems, and government/visa portals that do not accept modern WebP files.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting WebP to JPG?

Because JPG does not support transparency, transparent areas in the original WebP will automatically be rendered with a solid white background. To preserve transparency, convert to PNG instead.

Can I convert multiple WebP images at once?

Yes. You can drag and drop up to 20 WebP files at a time (up to 50 MB each) and convert them all simultaneously in a single session.
