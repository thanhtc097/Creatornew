Which image format should you choose?

Choose JPG for photos and compatibility

JPG is widely supported and works well for photographs. It usually creates smaller files by removing some visual data, so repeated editing and saving can reduce quality.

Choose PNG for transparency and graphics

PNG supports transparent backgrounds and preserves sharp edges, making it suitable for logos, screenshots, illustrations and interface graphics. Files can be larger than JPG or WebP.

Choose WebP for websites

WebP can deliver strong visual quality at a smaller size than older formats. It is a practical choice for blogs, portfolios and online stores where page speed matters.
