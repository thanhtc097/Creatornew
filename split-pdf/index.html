Frequently asked questions

Is Split PDF free to use?

Yes. CreatorNew Split PDF is completely free with no registration, no subscription limits, and no watermarks.

Are my PDF files uploaded to any server?

No. All PDF page extraction runs locally in your web browser's RAM sandbox. Zero documents are uploaded to CreatorNew.

How do I select which pages to extract?

You can enter page numbers and comma-separated ranges (such as 1-3, 5, 8-10) or click individual page thumbnails directly in the interactive grid.

Does splitting a PDF damage vector text or links?

No. The tool extracts original vector streams losslessly, preserving searchable text, internal hyperlinks, and crisp vector fonts.

Can I extract pages from password-protected PDFs?

No. You must remove password encryption before loading the document for in-browser processing.
