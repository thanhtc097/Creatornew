Frequently asked questions

Is Rotate PDF free?

Yes. CreatorNew Rotate PDF is completely free with no registration, watermarks, or page limits.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. All PDF processing and rotation happens entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

Can I rotate individual pages or the entire PDF?

Both. You can click any individual page thumbnail to rotate it clockwise, or use the 'Rotate All Left' and 'Rotate All Right' buttons to rotate every page simultaneously.

Does rotating PDF pages reduce document quality or text sharpness?

No. The rotation adjusts the page orientation metadata and rendering matrices directly, preserving original vector text, fonts, images and layout without compression artifacts.
