Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about converting PNG to WebP.

Is the PNG to WebP converter free?

Yes. You can convert PNG images to WebP completely free without registration, credit cards, or watermarks.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server?

No. All conversions execute locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Zero data is uploaded to CreatorNew or third-party servers.

Does WebP preserve PNG transparent backgrounds?

Yes! WebP fully supports 8-bit alpha channel transparency just like PNG. Your cutouts, logos, and stickers remain perfectly transparent with crisp edges.

How much file size reduction can I expect from PNG to WebP?

Converting lossless PNGs to WebP typically reduces file size by 60% to 85% with no perceptible loss in visual clarity.

Are WebP images supported in all web browsers?

Yes. Modern versions of Google Chrome, Apple Safari (iOS & macOS), Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Opera all support WebP natively (over 97% global market share).
