Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Is CreatorNew AI Image Upscaler really free?

Yes, completely free. There are no credit quotas, subscriptions, watermarks, or paywalls. You can upscale as many images as you need.

How does on-device upscaling protect my privacy?

Our super-resolution pipeline runs in a client-side Web Worker thread right on your device. Your sensitive photos, documents, and portraits never leave your computer or get transmitted across the internet.

Can this tool restore old or blurry family photos?

Yes! Use the Face & Portrait Recovery or Ultra Clarity preset with a 4X factor. It reconstructs facial features, sharpens eyes, and smooths out film grain or JPEG compression artifacts.

What is the difference between 2X, 4X, and 8X?

2X doubles the pixel width and height (4X total pixels), ideal for converting 1080p images to 4K. 4X quadruples the dimensions (16X total pixels), which is best for small crops or older digital camera pictures. 8X produces massive resolutions suitable for large physical prints and posters.
