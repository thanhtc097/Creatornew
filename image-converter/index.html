Preserving Transparency

How to handle transparent backgrounds during conversion

Avoid common conversion pitfalls like blackened or white-filled backgrounds when converting graphic assets.

⚠️

The JPG Transparency Trap

JPG does not have an alpha transparency channel. If you convert a transparent PNG logo or product cutout to JPG, the browser canvas fills all transparent pixels with solid white or black, ruining the transparent cutout.

✨

Switching PNG to WebP for Speed

WebP fully supports 8-bit alpha channel transparency while cutting PNG file size by up to 80%. If you have heavy transparent PNGs on your website, converting them to WebP gives you the best of both worlds.

🔍

Lossless vs Lossy Encoding

JPG always applies lossy quantization. PNG preserves every single pixel losslessly. When converting to WebP, modern browsers apply advanced prediction algorithms that keep fine details razor-sharp while discarding unnoticeable noise.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about browser-based image conversion.

Is CreatorNew Image Converter free?

Are my images uploaded to an external server?

No. Supported images are rendered and re-encoded locally in your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas APIs. Zero image files are ever uploaded across the network.

Which format should I convert to: JPG, PNG, or WebP?

Choose WebP for websites and blogs (smallest size with high visual quality). Choose PNG for graphics, screenshots, and transparent backgrounds. Choose JPG for photographs and legacy system compatibility.

Does converting PNG to JPG preserve transparent backgrounds?

No. The JPG specification does not support alpha channel transparency. Converting a transparent PNG to JPG will fill the background with solid white or black. To preserve transparency, convert to WebP or keep PNG.

Why is WebP recommended for modern websites?

WebP produces files that are 25% to 35% smaller than equivalent JPGs and up to 80% smaller than PNGs while supporting both 24-bit RGB color and 8-bit alpha transparency. It is supported by over 97% of modern web browsers.

Can I convert multiple images simultaneously?

Yes. You can batch convert up to 20 images per session with up to 50 MB per file, all processed concurrently right in your browser.
