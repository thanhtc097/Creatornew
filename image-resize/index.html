Best Practices

How to resize images without losing quality

Avoid pixelation, blur, and accidental aspect ratio distortion with these key principles.

1

Downscale Rather than Upscale

Downscaling (shrinking a 4000px camera photo to 1200px) preserves razor-sharp details because the browser blends adjacent pixels. Upscaling (enlarging a 400px thumbnail to 2000px) forces the computer to invent missing data, causing blur and pixelation.

2

Lock Proportions to Avoid Distortion

Always keep aspect ratio locking enabled unless your design specifically calls for an intentional stretch. If an original photo has a 3:2 ratio, changing it to 1:1 without cropping will squish your subjects horizontally or vertically.

3

Pixels for Screens, DPI for Print

For websites, social media, and digital presentations, only the exact pixel dimensions matter (e.g. 1920 × 1080). DPI (dots per inch) is merely a print metadata tag that indicates physical printing density on paper.

Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about resizing images online.

Is CreatorNew Image Resizer free?

Yes. You can resize JPG, PNG and WebP images completely free with no registration, no subscription fees, and no watermark added.

Are my images uploaded to any remote server?

No. Supported images are rendered and rescaled locally in your browser RAM using HTML5 Canvas. No image data is ever transferred across the internet.

How do I resize an image without distorting or squishing it?

Keep the "Maintain aspect ratio" option enabled. Entering either a target width or height will automatically calculate the proportional opposite dimension to prevent stretching.

What are the standard social media image sizes in 2026?

Popular dimensions include 1080x1080 (Instagram Square), 1080x1350 (Instagram Portrait 4:5), 1080x1920 (Instagram Reels & TikTok Stories 9:16), 1280x720 (YouTube Thumbnails), and 1200x630 (Facebook & OpenGraph link shares).

What is the difference between resizing pixels and compressing file size?

Resizing changes the physical pixel width and height (e.g., from 4000x3000 to 1200x900). Compressing reduces the file byte weight (e.g., from 5MB to 200KB) through quantization without necessarily altering the pixel dimensions.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes. You can batch upload up to 20 images at a time and resize them in a single batch session directly in your browser.
