Frequently asked questions

Is CreatorNew Image Compressor free?

Yes. You can compress supported images for free without creating an account or adding a watermark.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Supported images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to CreatorNew.

Which image formats are supported?

The converter supports JPG, PNG and WebP input and output in modern browsers.

Can I convert several images at once?

Yes. You can add up to 20 images per session, with a maximum size of 50 MB per image.

Why can a compressed image be larger than the original?

The original may already be optimized or saved at a lower quality than the level you selected. PNG is lossless, so re-encoding it may not reduce its size. Try WebP output for PNG, or use Balanced or Strong for JPG and WebP. Always check the displayed before-and-after size.
