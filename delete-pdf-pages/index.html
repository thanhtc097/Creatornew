Frequently asked questions

Is Delete PDF Pages free to use?

Yes. CreatorNew Delete PDF Pages is completely free and requires no account, subscription, or credit card.

Are my PDF documents uploaded to any server?

No. Your documents are parsed and modified locally in your browser's private memory sandbox. Files never travel across the internet.

How do I select pages to delete?

You can either click directly on page thumbnails in the interactive grid, or enter page numbers and ranges (e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-10) in the range input field.

Can I preview the cleaned PDF before downloading?

Yes. Once processed, an embedded preview lets you inspect the document to ensure the correct pages were removed before saving.

Does deleting pages alter the vector sharpness of remaining pages?

No. Remaining pages are preserved losslessly as native vector streams, keeping fonts sharp and selectable.
