Help & FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about merging PDFs privately.

Is CreatorNew Merge PDF free to use?

Yes. You can combine up to 20 PDF documents completely free with no registration, no subscription limits, and no watermark added.

Are my sensitive PDF documents uploaded to a cloud server?

No. Your PDF files are read, parsed, and merged entirely in your browser's local RAM. Files never leave your device or travel across any network.

How does CreatorNew compare to cloud PDF mergers like iLovePDF or Smallpdf?

Cloud mergers upload your sensitive documents (tax forms, legal contracts, medical records) to third-party servers. CreatorNew executes 100% on-device, ensuring strict confidentiality and compliance with privacy standards.

Does merging PDFs degrade text sharpness or image quality?

No. Pages are copied losslessly as vector PDF streams. Text remains crisp and searchable, vector curves remain sharp, and original embedded image resolutions are fully preserved.

Can I arrange or reorder documents before merging?

Yes. Once you add your PDFs, use the arrow buttons to move documents left or right to set your exact desired page sequence before generating the final combined file.

Can I merge password-protected PDF files?

No. For security and in-browser processing reasons, you must remove password encryption from your files before combining them.
