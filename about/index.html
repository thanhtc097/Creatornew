Our Mission & Technology

Free, Private & On-Device Tools for Creators

CreatorNew was founded on a simple principle: you should never have to upload confidential files or private photos to a remote cloud server just to convert a format, resize an image, or merge a PDF.

Published by the CreatorNew Engineering Team

The Problem with Traditional Online Tools

Every day, millions of creators, developers, educators, and professionals use online utility websites to perform routine digital tasks: compressing photos, converting graphics to modern formats like WebP, merging sensitive contract PDFs, or removing backgrounds. However, the majority of existing online tools come with serious drawbacks:

  • Privacy and Security Risks: Your files—often containing sensitive financial records, private selfies, legal contracts, or unreleased commercial graphics—are uploaded to remote third-party cloud storage where they could be logged, analyzed, or leaked.
  • Aggressive Paywalls & Subscriptions: Many tools offer 1–2 free uses before locking features behind expensive recurring subscriptions ($15–$30/month).
  • Watermarks & Artificial Limits: Competitors frequently watermark your output, downgrade resolution, or enforce artificial queue wait times unless you pay.
  • Sluggish Cloud Uploads: Processing large files requires uploading tens of megabytes over your internet connection, processing them on an overloaded server queue, and downloading them back.
The CreatorNew Alternative: We built CreatorNew from the ground up to operate 100% inside your browser. Your files are never sent across the network to our servers. Your processor and RAM do the work locally, safely, and instantaneously.

The Engineering Behind On-Device Processing

Modern web browsers have evolved into full-fledged operating platforms. CreatorNew harnesses advanced browser APIs and compiled WebAssembly to deliver professional-grade image manipulation and AI capabilities right on your device.

1. WebAssembly (WASM) for High-Performance Execution

WebAssembly enables low-level compiled code (written in C, C++, or Rust) to execute inside web browsers at near-native speeds. Instead of relying on interpreted JavaScript for complex pixel math, CreatorNew uses WebAssembly modules to handle heavy image codecs and document manipulation. This allows instantaneous batch conversions without heating up a cloud server or costing you data bandwidth.

2. ONNX Runtime Web & Local AI Models

Our flagship AI Background & Object Remover runs the state-of-the-art OpenCV LaMa neural network directly inside your browser using onnxruntime-web. When you load the tool, the quantized neural network model is downloaded to your browser cache once. Subsequent AI inferences run completely offline on your local device CPU via multi-threaded WebAssembly. No photos are ever sent to an external AI API.

3. Dedicated Web Workers & Zero UI Freezes

To ensure your browser interface remains silky smooth while processing multi-page PDFs or high-resolution camera photos, all data-intensive operations execute inside dedicated Web Workers in the background. Your user experience remains responsive, with live progress tracking and zero freezing.

4. In-Memory Canvas & Ephemeral Lifecycle

When you select an image or PDF, it is read into your browser's local RAM as an ephemeral ArrayBuffer or HTML5 Canvas element. Once you close the tab or press "Clear", that memory is automatically reclaimed by your browser's garbage collector. Zero traces remain on any external server.
